# bioastro-lab
Open projects at the intersection of biomedical engineering, space health, and extreme-environment biology.
BioAstro Lab is undertaking a 21-day public build to investigate and respond to seven real industry challenges at the intersection of space health, biomedical engineering, and extreme-environment biology.
Each case study will include:
- A clearly defined operational or research problem
- Evidence sources, assumptions, and limitations
- A proposed workflow, prototype, model, or decision-support concept
- A documented validation or feedback attempt
- A public, reproducible project record

The goal is to develop practical problem-framing, research, product-design, and technical communication capabilities while contributing transparent early-stage concepts to the space-health ecosystem.
